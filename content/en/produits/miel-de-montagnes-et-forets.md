## Mountains and forests

The **mountain and forest areas of the West and North-West** are exceptionally biodiverse. This multifloral honey captures all of that aromatic richness.

## Raw, local, traceable

Like our whole range, it comes from identified producers and is not processed: simply filtered and jarred.
