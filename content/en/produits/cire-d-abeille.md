## A precious by-product

A by-product of the hive, beeswax is a **white, yellow or brown**, odourless material. We turn it into **22 kg blocks** or **1 to 5 kg** formats.

Cosmetics, candles, care products, beekeeping: our annual capacity of around **200 tonnes** supports industrial volumes and export.
