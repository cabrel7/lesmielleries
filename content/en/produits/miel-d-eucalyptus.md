## The scent of the highlands

Eucalyptus trees in the West and North-West regions give bees an abundant bloom. The result is a fresh, woody honey, a favourite in hot drinks.

## Controlled quality

Filtered and packed in Douala, our eucalyptus honey undergoes no processing: it stays raw, just as the hive made it.
