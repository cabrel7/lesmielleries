## An exceptional, protected honey

Oku white honey is harvested on the **North-West** highlands around Mount Oku, in a village with **PGI** status (Protected Geographical Indication) and an approved specification.

Its ivory colour and creamy texture make it one of the most distinctive honeys in Africa. It comes from high-altitude mountain-forest blossoms, far from cities and industrial areas.

## Our commitment

Like all our honeys, it is **filtered, never heated or processed**, so it keeps all of its natural qualities. Purchase prices are agreed jointly with our partner beekeepers.
