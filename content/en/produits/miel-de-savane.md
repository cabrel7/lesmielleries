## Born in the savannah

Harvested in the **savannah zone (North – Adamawa)**, this honey reflects a varied, sun-drenched flora. Hives are set far from cities and industrial areas, in a healthy environment.

## Raw and local

Our honey is **not processed**: it is simply filtered and then packed in Douala. Its generous taste makes it the perfect partner for hot drinks and baking.
