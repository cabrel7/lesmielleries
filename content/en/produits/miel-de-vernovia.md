## An iconic plant

**Vernonia amygdalina**, known across Cameroon as **ndolé**, blooms in the West region. Bees turn it into a rare monofloral honey with a strong character.

## From hive to table

Harvested by partner beekeepers organised in cooperatives, it is filtered and packed by us, never heated, nothing added.
