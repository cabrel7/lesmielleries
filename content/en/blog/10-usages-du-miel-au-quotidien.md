Honey isn't just a treat: it's a versatile ingredient that fits in from breakfast to dinner.

## In drinks

1. **Tea, coffee, herbal tea**: a spoonful of honey replaces sugar and adds flavour. Our [eucalyptus honey](/en/products/miel-d-eucalyptus) is perfect in herbal tea.
2. **Lemon water**: honey, warm water and a squeeze of lemon for a natural, refreshing drink.
3. **Smoothies and fruit juices**: a drizzle of honey is all you need.

## In baking

4. **Doughnuts**: add honey to the batter or glaze them straight out of the oil.
5. **Bread and cakes**: replace some of the sugar with honey for a softer crumb.
6. **Homemade biscuits**: honey gives them a lovely golden colour.

## In savoury cooking

7. **Marinades**: honey, garlic, ginger and chilli, ideal for grilled chicken or fish.
8. **Dressings**: a touch of honey balances acidity.

## At breakfast

9. **Yoghurt, porridge, cereal**: honey sweetens naturally.
10. **Toast**: on warm bread, nothing could be simpler. Try our [Savannah honey](/en/products/miel-de-savane)!

## Tip

To replace sugar with honey in a recipe, use about **two thirds** of the amount of sugar: honey is sweeter.
